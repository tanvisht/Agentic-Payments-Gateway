ABOUT AGENTPAY
What is AgentPay?

A payment gateway built for AI agents. You delegate bounded spending authority once, as a signed mandate. Your agent can then buy on your behalf through any connected rail: cards via Stripe, on-chain escrow, or x402 stablecoins. Every payment is checked against your mandate and a risk engine before money moves.

Does the agent ever see my card?

No. You save a card once through a Stripe SetupIntent in your own browser. The agent only ever holds its own signing key and a narrow set of tools: search, check mandate, buy an offer. It cannot even choose a raw amount, only a merchant-signed offer price.

What stops a runaway or hijacked agent?

Five independent layers: request signatures with single-use nonces, the signed mandate (limits, merchants, categories, expiry), atomic budget reservation, a risk engine tuned to agent failure modes (velocity, duplicates, near-limit amounts, low reputation), and escalation to you above any threshold you choose.

RAILS & MONEY
Which payment providers are supported?

Stripe for cards today, plus an on-chain escrow rail (Solidity, USDC) and x402 stablecoin payments. New processors implement six methods behind one interface. The gateway handles routing, circuit breakers and failover on retryable errors.

How is money accounted for?

Every movement is a balanced double-entry journal. Balances are derived, never stored, and the trial balance must always be zero. Property-based tests throw thousands of random payment, capture, cancel and refund sequences at the gateway to prove it.

Can I see everything my agent did?

Yes. The per-mandate audit shows what you authorized next to every attempt the agent made, including ones that were blocked. It re-checks each payment against your signed scope and reports what the agent's reasoning cost in LLM tokens.
